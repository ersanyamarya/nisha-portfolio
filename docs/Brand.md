# Nisha Kumari - Brand Guidelines

**Version:** v1 **Status:** Final **Date:** August 25, 2026

## 1. Brand Heart

### Mission Statement

To craft seamless, research-backed digital experiences with the precision of a master barista—balancing deep user empathy (UX) with meticulous visual craft (UI).

### Vision Statement

To bring a bold, refreshing flavor to the European tech scene by designing frictionless user journeys grounded in behavioral insights, elevated by engaging spatial storytelling.

### Core Values

- **The Source (UX Research & Strategy):** Great coffee starts at the farm. Great design starts with the user. Empathy, user interviews, and data form our foundation.
- **The Extraction (Information Architecture & Flow):** Calibrating the perfect pressure. We map seamless user journeys and logical flows that remove friction and guide the user intuitively.
- **The Latte Art (Delight & UI Craft):** The finishing touch. Elevating the validated UX through stunning UI, spatial interactions, and aesthetic joy inspired by a background in fine art painting.

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Audience

Hiring Managers and Design Directors at top European tech companies looking for a Senior Product Designer. Their biggest pain point is finding a "unicorn" who truly excels at both rigorous UX strategy and premium UI craft without compromising either.

### Secondary Audience

Recruiters screening for top-tier talent, and fellow designers seeking inspiration.

### The "Aha!" Moment

The exact moment they interact with the coffee bean character seamlessly guiding them through a complex UX research case study—proving your strategic depth is matched only by your high-end visual execution.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality/Archetype

A curated blend of **The Sage** (analytical, research-driven, guiding the user) and **The Creator** (innovative, artistic, crafting perfection).

### Voice Attributes

- Warm
- Precise
- Crafty

### Tone Matrix (Do's and Don'ts)

| In this situation | We sound like this (Do) | We never sound like this (Don't) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Writing a Case Study** | Focus on user pain points, metrics, and methodical problem-solving. Use subtle brewing analogies. | Hide behind corporate jargon or overdo coffee puns to the point of distraction. |
| **About Me Page** | Highlight passion for painting, travel, and barista craft. Sound warm, inviting, and cultured. | Sound sterile, overly formal, or read like a standard LinkedIn resume. |
| **UI Micro-copy** | Be concise, helpful, and slightly delightful—like a friendly barista. | Be robotic or leave the user guessing what happens next. |

## 4. Core Messaging

### Elevator Pitch

A premium, coffee-inspired UX design portfolio that blends meticulous digital craftsmanship with a warm, interactive aesthetic to showcase high-tier design work.

### Value Proposition / Taglines

- **Primary Tagline:** Precision in the process. Delight in the details.
- **Supporting Tagline:** I am Nisha Kumari, a Berlin-based Product Designer crafting intuitive, research-backed products with an artist's touch.

### Key Messaging Pillars

1. **Data-Driven Roasting (Strategic UX):** Grounding every design decision in deep user research and behavioral insights to ensure the foundation is strong.
2. **The Perfect Pour (High-Fidelity UI):** Translating complex workflows into beautiful, accessible, and tactile visual interfaces.
3. **The European Blend (Empathy & Culture):** Drawing on a love for travel and art to build inclusive, global-minded digital products.

## 5. Visual Identity Guardrails

### Logo Usage

A minimalist typographic wordmark ("Nisha.") or a clean "NK" monogram. We avoid literal coffee cups in the logo to keep it strictly professional, letting the interactive character do the thematic lifting in the UI.

### UI & Interface Design (Aesthetic)

A **"Third-Wave Berlin Roastery"** aesthetic. Clean, airy, expansive layouts with generous white space. We use soft glassmorphism for floating UI cards to create a tactile, layered feel that allows spatial character animations to blend seamlessly without the background feeling cluttered. Shadows are soft, diffused, and slightly tinted with a warm coffee hue rather than harsh blacks.

### Iconography

Minimalist, monoline icons with a consistent 1.5px stroke weight. Rounded joints keep it approachable (Warm). For inactive states, we use a muted grey-brown; for active states, the stroke turns to our primary accent color with a subtle drop shadow.

### Typography Scale

**Primary Typeface:** Playfair Display (Display/Headings) **Secondary Typeface:** Inter (Body/UI Labels)

| Element       | Typeface & Weight         | Styling Notes                         | Usage                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **H1**        | Playfair Display, Medium  | 64px, high impact                     | Primary tagline, Hero sections |
| **H2**        | Playfair Display, Regular | 32px, elegant                         | Case study and section headers |
| **Body**      | Inter, Regular            | 16px, clean and highly legible        | Paragraphs, case study text    |
| **UI Labels** | Inter, Medium             | 14px, ALL CAPS, slight letter spacing | Buttons, Navigation, Metadata  |

### Full Color System

A sophisticated palette inspired by coffee roasting, optimized for high-contrast accessibility in both Light and Dark modes.

**Backgrounds & Surfaces (The Layers)**

> _Surface styling note: Card/Surface Bg uses `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` for a frosted glass effect._

| Element             | Light Mode                 | Dark Mode                 | Usage                        |
| ------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **App Background**  | `#FDFBF7` (Oat Milk)       | `#1C1917` (Deep Espresso) | Main portfolio canvas        |
| **Card/Surface Bg** | `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` | `rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.6)`   | Floating UI cards, nav bars  |
| **Borders**         | `#EBE5DF` (Soft Crema)     | `#3D3834` (Burnt Ash)     | Dividers, card edges, inputs |

**Typography Colors (The Structure)**

| Element       | Light Mode             | Dark Mode            | Usage                           |
| ------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Headings**  | `#292524` (Dark Roast) | `#FDFBF7` (Oat Milk) | All H-level typography          |
| **Body Text** | `#57534E` (Macchiato)  | `#D6D3D1` (Warm Ash) | Paragraphs, captions, subtitles |

**Brand Accents & Semantic Signals (The Interactions)**

> _Accent usage note: Use accents sparingly to guide the user's eye to primary actions and character highlights._

| Signal      | Hex Code  | Color Name              | Usage                                    |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Primary** | `#C2785B` | Terracotta / Warm Roast | Buttons, Links, Character Highlights     |
| **Error**   | `#B91C1C` | Crimson                 | Form validation, missing fields          |
| **Success** | `#4D7C5F` | Matcha Green            | Form submissions, positive confirmations |

### Imagery & Photography Style

A mix of immaculate, high-fidelity UI mockups and warm, candid photography. Personal photos (traveling Europe, painting, making coffee) should have a slightly warm, cinematic grade to match the UI. The interactive coffee bean character features spatial animations reacting to user scroll depth and cursor placement, maintaining a premium, tactile finish that is clever but never cartoonish or goofy.
